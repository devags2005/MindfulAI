# 🧠 MindfulAI – Your AI Wellness Friend

Hi! I built a fun and helpful AI chatbot called **MindfulAI** using PartyRock (by Amazon AWS).  
It talks to you nicely and helps when you're feeling sad, stressed, or just want to share your thoughts.

## 💡 What it does:
- Understands how you're feeling
- Replies with friendly and kind messages
- Gives tips to feel better (like breathing or journaling)
- No coding needed — built with PartyRock AI blocks!
